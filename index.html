Must-haves and Nice-to-haves
Must-haves
  • Accessibility: open access for all parties, at reasonable cost
  • Validity: remaining capacity or SoH
  • Verifiability: battery ID + test date
  • Comparability: same metrics across brands and testers
  • Interpretability: remaining range, replacement need
  • Usable in private sales
  • Coverage of older battery systems
  • Compliance: EU Battery Regulation, GDPR
Nice-to-haves
  • Power and safety indicators
  • Stated test method and precision
  • Independent third-party testing
  • Battery health history
  • Independence from a single manufacturer
  • Integration into listing platforms
  • Use for insurance and financing
  • Use at lease end
